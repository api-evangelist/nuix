# Nuix

> Nuix is a leading global provider of investigative analytics and intelligence software. With over 25 years of experience, Nuix empowers law enforcement, legal firms, government departments, and large corporations worldwide to find truth in the digital world by transforming vast amounts of unstructured data into actionable insights with speed, scalability, and forensic accuracy.

Nuix is headquartered in Australia and operates 11 offices globally across North America, EMEA, and APAC. The company has invested $200+ million in R&D since 2008, employs 170+ developers, and holds 7 key patents. Nuix is trusted by over 1,800 organizations globally, including 90+ regulators worldwide, and is supported by 150+ partners. The company is publicly listed and driven by a mission to be a "Force for Good."

## Products

### Nuix Neo Platform
- [Nuix Neo Platform Overview](https://www.nuix.com/nuix-neo): The unified platform powered by the patented Nuix Engine. Processes 1,000+ file types to transform unstructured data into structured, searchable assets. Includes advanced AI for pattern recognition, automated workflows, and customizable solution modules.

### Nuix Neo Solutions
- [Nuix Neo Legal](https://www.nuix.com/solutions/neo-legal-ediscovery): eDiscovery solution for law firms, legal service providers, government, and corporates. Enables faster, easier, and smarter eDiscovery with AI-driven insights.
- [Nuix Neo Discover](https://www.nuix.com/solutions/neo-discover): AI-powered responsive review platform. Combines Nuix Discover's industry-leading review with Nuix Neo capabilities. Powered by proprietary Cognitive AI (CogAI), AI-enabled search, and 500+ pre-built eDiscovery-tuned models. Deployable in cloud or on-premises.
- [Nuix Neo Discover On-Premises](https://www.nuix.com/solutions/neo-discover-on-prem): Industry-leading on-premises review platform with advanced AI capabilities.
- [Nuix Neo Investigations](https://www.nuix.com/solutions/fraud-investigations): Fraud and investigations solution for connecting the dots in complex cases. Enables rapid data ingestion and collaborative fraud analysis techniques.
- [Nuix Neo Data Privacy](https://www.nuix.com/solutions/data-privacy): Data privacy management platform for identifying and protecting sensitive information. Handles data breach readiness, breach notifications, FOI/DSAR responses, and PII management.
- [Nuix Neo Foundation](https://www.nuix.com/solutions/neo-foundation): Core data processing platform for mastering complex data challenges.

### Nuix Components & Technology
- [Nuix Workstation](https://www.nuix.com/technology/nuix-workstation): Desktop application for forensic data processing and analysis.
- [Nuix Investigate](https://www.nuix.com/technology/nuix-investigate): Investigation and intelligence analysis tool.
- [Nuix Enterprise Collection Centre](https://www.nuix.com/technology/nuix-enterprise-collection-center): Enterprise-scale data collection solution.
- [Nuix Adaptive Security](https://www.nuix.com/solutions/adaptive-security): Security intelligence and threat detection solution.
- [Nuix Rampiva](https://www.nuix.com/technology/nuix-rampiva): Automation and workflow management for large-scale Nuix deployments.
